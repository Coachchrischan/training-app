**Team maths for a full room:**

- 10 pairs = 10 machines in play, 14 spare. Easiest to run.
- 6 trios = 6 machines per bank. Exactly matches the room. The default.
- 5 quads = 5 machines per bank, 1 spare for a swap-out.
- 4 teams of 5 = deep benches, best for chase and capture formats.

## What "engaging" means here

> Nobody sits on a machine grinding alone for long stretches. Somebody is always about to move, and the reason they move is another person.

That definition produces rules, and every session in this library obeys them:

1. No single unbroken effort longer than 90 seconds on one machine.
2. Every swap is triggered by a person or a target, never only by a clock the members cannot see.
3. A visible score changes at least every 3 minutes.
4. The format changes at least twice across the session, so it never becomes one long grind.
5. Everyone finishes within about 2 minutes of each other. Handicap the fast teams rather than stretching the slow ones.
6. Zero gear moves once the class starts. Everything is set before the door opens.
7. Anyone can be told their lane one to one. Never announce a downgrade to the room.

**Session shape:** 10 min brief and warm-up, 35 to 40 min of work in 2 or 3 parts, 5 min cool-down and scoreboard.

**Lanes**

- **Base** — Finishing the session in good shape matters more than the number. Halve holds, drop distances, use the ski instead of the runner.
- **Build** — The prescribed numbers. Most of the room sits here.
- **Push** — The top end. Only for people already holding pace with a full sentence left in them.

## The sessions

### GD-01 · Tag Team Hundreds

**Pairs** · 10 pairs · 36 min · *Tag relay*

**The hook.** One works, one holds. The hold is what makes you want them to hurry.

**Set-up.** 1 machine per pair. Pairs 1-4 rower, 5-7 bike, 8-10 ski.

**How it runs**

- Part A (16 min): pair chips at 100 cal on their machine. Partner holds a front rack dumbbell hold. Swap on the tag, no fixed interval, but nobody may hold longer than 60 sec.
- Part B (16 min): identical, but the machine changes to the bank you did not use and the hold becomes a wall sit.

**Score.** Total calories banked by the pair across both parts.

**Lanes.** Base: 80 cal, hold 30 sec max. Build: 100 cal. Push: 130 cal.

**Coach notes**

- Call the 60 second hold ceiling loudly. It is the safety valve.
- Pairs that finish early go again from zero, they do not stop.

*Gear: rower, bike, ski, dumbbell.*

### GD-02 · Two On, One Off

**Trios** · 6 trios · 38 min · *Tag relay*

**The hook.** Two people are always working, one is always recovering. The rest is earned by whoever hits their target first.

**Set-up.** Each trio owns 1 rower and 1 ski, side by side.

**How it runs**

- Trio holds a rower and a ski. Two work, one rests.
- First person to hit their target (250 m row / 200 m ski) taps out, the rester takes their machine, the finisher rests.
- Run it for 3 blocks of 10 min with 2 min between. Block 2 swaps ski for bike, block 3 swaps rower for runner.

**Score.** Total swaps completed by the trio. Shout the running count each block.

**Lanes.** Base: 200 m row / 150 m ski. Build: 250 m row / 200 m ski. Push: 300 m row / 250 m ski.

**Coach notes**

- This is the format Chris described. It self-paces: push too hard and you just buy yourself more work.
- Watch for a trio where one person never rests. Force a rotation.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, runner.*

### GD-03 · Hot Potato

**Trios** · 6 trios · 32 min · *Tag relay*

**The hook.** The machine is the potato. You cannot hold it for more than 45 seconds.

**Set-up.** 1 machine per trio, 1 kettlebell, 1 box.

**How it runs**

- AMRAP 14: trio accumulates calories on one machine. Hard 45 second ceiling per person, then it must pass on.
- The two off the machine work through a shared set of 60 KB swings and 40 box step-overs. When the floor work is done the trio banks a bonus 20 cal.
- Rest 3 min. Repeat for 14 on a different machine, floor work becomes 60 slam balls and 40 lunges.

**Score.** Calories plus bonuses.

**Lanes.** Base: 30 sec ceiling, 40/30 floor reps. Build: as written. Push: 45 sec ceiling, 80/50 floor reps.

**Coach notes**

- Use a 45 second interval beep on the big screen. It does the coaching for you.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, kettlebell, box.*

### GD-04 · Rolling Relay

**Quads** · 5 quads · 40 min · *Tag relay*

**The hook.** Four people, four jobs, everyone rotates one place every time the rower finishes.

**Set-up.** Each quad owns a rower, a ski, a bike and a box.

**How it runs**

- Positions: rower (250 m), ski (200 m), bike (20 cal), box (max step-overs, this is the rest).
- When the rower finishes their 250 m everyone rotates one position clockwise. The rower becomes the box.
- 3 blocks of 11 min, 2 min between. Block 2 the trigger becomes the ski, block 3 the trigger becomes the bike.

**Score.** Full rotations completed, plus total box step-overs as the tiebreak.

**Lanes.** Base: 200 m / 150 m / 15 cal. Build: as written. Push: 300 m / 250 m / 25 cal.

**Coach notes**

- Moving the trigger between blocks completely changes who suffers. Say that out loud, people enjoy it.
- The box position is genuinely the rest. Tell them to keep it honest and light.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, box.*

### GD-05 · Baton

**Quads** · 5 quads · 34 min · *Tag relay*

**The hook.** You cannot start your machine until the dumbbell is physically in your hand.

**Set-up.** Each quad owns one machine per bank plus a dumbbell as the baton.

**How it runs**

- The quad must cover 4000 m row, 3000 m ski, 3000 m bike as a team, in that order.
- Only one person works at a time. Handover is a dumbbell farmers carry across the room and back, then the baton is passed.
- Cap 30 min. Whatever is left is the deficit.

**Score.** Time to finish, or metres remaining at the cap.

**Lanes.** Base: 3000/2000/2000. Build: as written. Push: 5000/4000/4000.

**Coach notes**

- The carry is the whole trick. It stops people hiding and it gives the room something to watch.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, dumbbell.*

### GD-06 · Cat and Mouse

**Trios** · 6 trios · 30 min · *Chase*

**The hook.** You are chasing the trio in front. If you catch them you take their seat and they drop to last.

**Set-up.** 6 rowers in a line, numbered 1 to 6. Trios start staggered.

**How it runs**

- All 6 rowers running, one athlete per trio on at a time, swap every 250 m.
- Trios start on a 20 second stagger. If your trio's cumulative metres pass the trio ahead, you swap rowers and the standings change on the board.
- 3 rounds of 8 min, 2 min between. Round 2 on the bikes, round 3 on the skis.

**Score.** Finishing position each round. 6 points for first down to 1 for last.

**Lanes.** Base: swap every 150 m. Build: 250 m. Push: 350 m.

**Coach notes**

- Put the standings on the TV. That is the entire session.
- Physically moving seats feels dramatic and takes 5 seconds. Worth it.

*Gear: rower, bike, ski.*

### GD-07 · Catch the Team in Front

**Quads** · 5 quads · 36 min · *Chase*

**The hook.** Classic chipper chase. Catch the quad in front and they owe you a penalty.

**Set-up.** Circuit of 5 stations: rower, ski, bike, sled lane, box and dumbbell corner.

**How it runs**

- Quads start at a different station, staggered. Station work: 500 m row / 400 m ski / 30 cal bike / 2 sled lengths / 60 box step-overs, shared however the quad likes.
- Chase for 30 min. If you arrive at a station that is still occupied, the occupying team owes 10 burpees at the end.
- Final 2 min: every team stops and completes any owed burpees.

**Score.** Stations completed, minus owed burpees at 3 per station equivalent.

**Lanes.** Base: reduce all station numbers by a third. Build: as written. Push: add 25 percent.

**Coach notes**

- Only 2 sled lanes, so stagger starts so no more than 2 teams can be at the sled at once.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, sled, box, dumbbell.*

### GD-08 · Pursuit Ladder

**Pairs** · 10 pairs · 32 min · *Chase*

**The hook.** The ladder gets shorter as you get more tired, so the room speeds up instead of slowing down.

**Set-up.** 1 machine per pair.

**How it runs**

- Ladder of 400, 350, 300, 250, 200, 150, 100 m. Partners alternate rungs, no rest between.
- Anyone who finishes the full ladder starts again at 400.
- 20 min cap. Then 8 min of pairs alternating max cal, 30 sec on 30 sec off.

**Score.** Rungs cleared, then calories in the finisher.

**Lanes.** Base: start at 250. Build: 400. Push: start at 500.

**Coach notes**

- Everybody finishes on a sprint. That is why the session feels good rather than long.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike.*

### GD-09 · Up and Down

**Trios** · 6 trios · 38 min · *Ladder*

**The hook.** One climbs the ladder, one descends it, one holds. You meet in the middle.

**Set-up.** Each trio owns a rower and a bike.

**How it runs**

- Athlete A climbs 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 cal on the rower. Athlete B descends 50, 40, 30, 20, 10 cal on the bike. Athlete C holds a plank or a wall sit and is released only when both A and B finish a rung.
- Rotate one position after each full rung. 3 full ladders, 12 min cap each, 2 min between.
- If the ladder is finished inside the cap, the trio banks the remaining time as bonus calories on any machine.

**Score.** Ladders completed plus bonus calories.

**Lanes.** Base: 5/10/15/20/25. Build: as written. Push: 15/25/35/45/55.

**Coach notes**

- The holder gets released by other people's work. That is the bit members talk about afterwards.

*Gear: rower, bike.*

### GD-10 · Descending Trade

**Quads** · 5 quads · 34 min · *Ladder*

**The hook.** Every round you lose a machine, so the same work squeezes onto fewer stations.

**Set-up.** Each quad owns a rower, ski, bike and a wall ball.

**How it runs**

- Round 1 (10 min): all 3 machines live, quad accumulates 150 cal total.
- Round 2 (9 min): bike is out, 120 cal on 2 machines.
- Round 3 (8 min): ski is out, 90 cal on the rower only, the other 3 do wall balls in a shared set of 100.
- 2 min between rounds.

**Score.** Rounds completed inside the cap. Uncompleted calories carry as a deficit.

**Lanes.** Base: 120 / 90 / 60. Build: as written. Push: 200 / 160 / 120.

**Coach notes**

- The squeeze is the point. By round 3 there is genuine queueing pressure and people start coaching each other.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, wallball.*

### GD-11 · Pyramid Partners

**Pairs** · 10 pairs · 36 min · *Ladder*

**The hook.** Up and back down, and the peak is the only place you both work at once.

**Set-up.** 1 machine per pair plus a shared dumbbell.

**How it runs**

- 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 400, 300, 200, 100 m alternating partners.
- At the 500 m peak both partners work: one on the machine, one doing dumbbell thrusters until the 500 is done.
- Cap 34 min. Whoever finishes early repeats the 500 peak as a bonus.

**Score.** Time to complete, or metres left.

**Lanes.** Base: peak at 300. Build: peak at 500. Push: peak at 750.

**Coach notes**

- Call out the peak. It is the loudest 2 minutes of the session.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, dumbbell.*

### GD-12 · Deck of Pain

**Trios** · 6 trios · 35 min · *Random draw*

**The hook.** Nobody knows what is coming, so nobody paces.

**Set-up.** Each trio owns a machine and a card deck at their station.

**How it runs**

- Trio flips a card. Hearts = row metres, Diamonds = bike cal, Spades = ski metres, Clubs = floor work. Face cards = 10, Ace = 15.
- Card value times 10 for metres, times 1 for calories. One person works the card, the other two rest or hold.
- 3 blocks of 10 min, 2 min between. Aces are a full-trio effort.

**Score.** Cards cleared.

**Lanes.** Base: value times 5 for metres. Build: as written. Push: value times 15.

**Coach notes**

- Pre-sort the decks so no trio gets 4 aces in a row.
- Keep a spare deck. They get sweaty and stick together.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, dumbbell.*

### GD-13 · Dice Roulette

**Quads** · 5 quads · 34 min · *Random draw*

**The hook.** The die decides who works and on what. The room laughs every 90 seconds.

**Set-up.** One large die per quad.

**How it runs**

- Roll: 1 rower, 2 ski, 3 bike, 4 sled, 5 box and dumbbell, 6 quad's choice.
- Every roll is a 90 second max effort by one nominated person. The quad must rotate the worker each roll.
- 20 rolls or 30 min, whichever comes first.

**Score.** Total calories and metres, converted on the board (1 cal = 10 m).

**Lanes.** Base: 60 sec efforts. Build: 90 sec. Push: 2 min.

**Coach notes**

- Only 2 sled lanes: if a 4 is rolled and both lanes are busy, they re-roll. Say this at the brief.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, sled, box, dumbbell.*

### GD-14 · Wheel of Machines

**Trios** · 6 trios · 32 min · *Random draw*

**The hook.** The whole room moves at once to whatever the wheel says. Chaos, but controlled chaos.

**Set-up.** All banks live. A spinner on the TV picks the machine each round.

**How it runs**

- 8 rounds of 3 min with 1 min transition. Each round the wheel picks a machine.
- Within the round the trio splits the work however they like, but every person must touch the machine.
- Two rounds are 'floor' rounds: burpees, wall balls and box jumps, shared.

**Score.** Team total each round, running scoreboard.

**Lanes.** Base: 2 min rounds. Build: 3 min. Push: 4 min.

**Coach notes**

- Transition time is non negotiable at 1 min. Six trios moving to the same bank needs a beat.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, runner, wallball, box.*

### GD-15 · Buy In, Buy Out

**Quads** · 5 quads · 38 min · *Buy in*

**The hook.** You pay to start and you pay to leave. The middle is where you make it back.

**Set-up.** Each quad owns a rower, a ski and a floor square.

**How it runs**

- Buy in: 100 team burpees, shared, before anyone touches a machine.
- Middle (20 min): AMRAP of 250 m row, 200 m ski, 15 box step-overs, rotating one person at a time.
- Buy out: 100 team wall balls. Clock stops when the last one lands.

**Score.** Rounds in the middle. Teams that do not finish the buy out lose 2 rounds.

**Lanes.** Base: 60 rep buy in and out. Build: 100. Push: 150.

**Coach notes**

- Fast teams blow the buy in out and then die in the middle. Let them learn it once.

*Gear: rower, ski, box, wallball.*

### GD-16 · Toll Gate

**Pairs** · 10 pairs · 30 min · *Buy in*

**The hook.** Every 4 minutes the room stops and pays a toll together.

**Set-up.** 1 machine per pair, a shared toll station in the middle of the room.

**How it runs**

- Pairs alternate 250 m efforts on their machine for 4 min.
- On the horn every pair leaves their machine, runs to the toll station and completes 20 synchronised burpees together, then back.
- 6 gates, 28 min total.

**Score.** Metres between gates.

**Lanes.** Base: 12 synchro burpees. Build: 20. Push: 30.

**Coach notes**

- Synchronised means synchronised. It is the only part of the session where pairs have to look at each other.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike.*

### GD-17 · Team Total 3000

**Quads** · 5 quads · 36 min · *Accumulator*

**The hook.** One number on the board, and it only moves when somebody is working.

**Set-up.** Each quad owns a rower, ski and bike.

**How it runs**

- The quad must accumulate 3000 points. 1 m row = 1, 1 m ski = 1.2, 1 cal bike = 8.
- Max 2 people on machines at any time. The other 2 must be holding a plank, a wall sit or a farmers carry.
- 30 min cap.

**Score.** Time to 3000, or points at the cap.

**Lanes.** Base: 2200 points. Build: 3000. Push: 4000.

**Coach notes**

- Publish the conversion on the screen. Teams will argue about the optimal machine and that argument is engagement.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike.*

### GD-18 · Bank It

**Trios** · 6 trios · 34 min · *Accumulator*

**The hook.** Calories are not yours until you carry them to the bank. Cash out too late and you lose them.

**Set-up.** Each trio owns 1 machine, and there is a shared 'bank' station.

**How it runs**

- 4 rounds of 6 min. Trio accumulates calories, rotating every 45 sec.
- At any point one member may run to the bank and lock in the current total. Once banked it is safe.
- At the horn, anything not banked is lost. Rest 2 min between rounds.

**Score.** Banked calories only.

**Lanes.** Base: 30 sec rotations. Build: 45 sec. Push: 60 sec.

**Coach notes**

- The gamble is the whole session. Do not tell them the optimal strategy, let them find it by round 3.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike.*

### GD-19 · Points Grab

**Pairs** · 10 pairs · 32 min · *Accumulator*

**The hook.** Different machines are worth different points and the values flip halfway.

**Set-up.** All banks live, points values posted on each.

**How it runs**

- Part A (14 min): row 1 point per 100 m, ski 2 per 100 m, bike 1 per 5 cal, box 1 per 10 step-overs. Pairs alternate, free choice of station.
- Part B (14 min): the values invert. The cheap stations become the expensive ones.
- 2 min between.

**Score.** Total points.

**Lanes.** Base: all values doubled for base lane. Build: as written. Push: as written, no rest between partner swaps.

**Coach notes**

- Only 6 of each machine. Tell pairs they may have to queue and queueing costs points. It drives movement.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, box.*

### GD-20 · Capture the Erg

**Quads** · 5 quads · 36 min · *Capture*

**The hook.** You take a machine off another team by out-working them.

**Set-up.** 6 rowers in the middle of the room as the contested ground.

**How it runs**

- Each quad starts holding one rower. The sixth is neutral.
- A quad captures a machine by accumulating 500 m more than the team currently holding it, tracked on the board.
- 3 blocks of 10 min. Holding a machine at the horn is worth 3 points, holding two is worth 8.

**Score.** Points from machines held at each horn.

**Lanes.** Base: 350 m to capture. Build: 500 m. Push: 700 m.

**Coach notes**

- Needs a scorer. Use a member sitting out, or run it with 4 teams and coach the board yourself.
- The neutral machine gets fought over immediately. Good.

*Gear: rower.*

### GD-21 · King of the Rower

**Pairs** · 10 pairs · 30 min · *Capture*

**The hook.** Two pairs are on the throne, everyone else is trying to knock them off.

**Set-up.** Rowers 1 and 2 are the thrones. Everything else is the queue.

**How it runs**

- Two pairs hold the thrones. All other pairs work a challenger circuit: 20 cal bike, 15 box step-overs, 200 m ski.
- Finishing the circuit earns one challenge: a 250 m row race against a throne holder. Winner takes the throne.
- 28 min. A pair scores 1 point per minute on the throne.

**Score.** Throne minutes.

**Lanes.** Base: challenge is 150 m. Build: 250 m. Push: 350 m.

**Coach notes**

- This one is loud. Keep the challenger circuit short so challenges come thick and fast.

*Gear: rower, bike, ski, box.*

### GD-22 · Territory

**Trios** · 6 trios · 34 min · *Capture*

**The hook.** Own the zone at the horn and you keep it for the next round.

**Set-up.** The room is split into 6 zones, one per bank plus sled and floor.

**How it runs**

- 6 rounds of 4 min, 1 min transition. Each round a trio picks an unclaimed zone and works it.
- Owning a zone at the horn means a bonus multiplier next time you enter it.
- Trios cannot repeat a zone until all 6 have been visited.

**Score.** Zone points times multipliers.

**Lanes.** Base: 3 min rounds. Build: 4 min. Push: 5 min.

**Coach notes**

- Draw the zones on the whiteboard before class. Do not try to explain it verbally.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, sled, box, dumbbell.*

### GD-23 · Hold or Go

**Pairs** · 10 pairs · 32 min · *Partner hold*

**The hook.** Your partner is in a hold the entire time you are working. You decide how long they suffer.

**Set-up.** 1 machine per pair, one kettlebell.

**How it runs**

- 3 ladders of 9 min with 3 min between, as per the Friday Conditioning format.
- Ladder: 100, 150, 200, 250, 300 m. One works, one holds.
- Ladder 1 goblet hold, ladder 2 wall sit, ladder 3 glute bridge. Bike pairs wall sit on the rig uprights.

**Score.** Rungs cleared across three ladders.

**Lanes.** Base: halve the hold time, drop a rung. Build: as written. Push: add a 350 m rung.

**Coach notes**

- Straight lift of the Friday Conditioning mechanic the members already know. Good for a week when you want low explanation overhead.
- First leg over 75 seconds means drop that pair a lane for the next ladder.

*Gear: rower, bike, kettlebell.*

### GD-24 · Ransom

**Trios** · 6 trios · 33 min · *Partner hold*

**The hook.** One teammate is held hostage and the other two have to buy them out.

**Set-up.** 1 machine per trio, one box as the holding pen.

**How it runs**

- One member sits in a wall sit at the box. The other two must accumulate 40 cal on the machine to free them.
- Freed member immediately takes the machine, one of the workers becomes the hostage.
- 3 blocks of 9 min, 2 min between. Block 2 the ransom is 50 cal, block 3 it is 60.

**Score.** Ransoms paid.

**Lanes.** Base: 25 / 30 / 35 cal. Build: as written. Push: 50 / 65 / 80 cal.

**Coach notes**

- Hostage may sit on the box if the wall sit fails. Nobody should be in a failing hold for 2 minutes.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, box.*

### GD-25 · Frozen

**Quads** · 5 quads · 30 min · *Partner hold*

**The hook.** Finish your piece and you freeze. Nobody moves until the whole team is frozen.

**Set-up.** Each quad owns 2 machines and 2 floor squares.

**How it runs**

- Each round: 2 people on machines (300 m row, 250 m ski), 2 on the floor (25 wall balls, 20 box step-overs).
- Finish your job and you hold a plank until all 4 are done. Then rotate.
- 26 min AMRAP of rounds.

**Score.** Rounds completed.

**Lanes.** Base: 200 m / 150 m / 15 / 12. Build: as written. Push: 400 m / 350 m / 35 / 30.

**Coach notes**

- The fast one in the team finds out very quickly that they should have paced with the group. Great teaching session.

*Gear: rower, ski, wallball, box.*

### GD-26 · Six Station Carousel

**Trios** · 6 trios · 38 min · *Carousel*

**The hook.** Everyone moves at once, every 2 minutes, for the whole session. Pure rhythm.

**Set-up.** 6 stations around the room: rower, ski, bike, sled, box and dumbbell, wall ball.

**How it runs**

- E2MOM. 6 stations, 6 trios, one trio per station.
- Inside the 2 min the trio works 40 seconds each, no overlap.
- 3 laps of the carousel (36 min) with a 1 min breather after each lap.

**Score.** Team total per station, boarded per lap.

**Lanes.** Base: 30 sec each with 10 sec changeover. Build: 40 sec each. Push: 40 sec each, heavier loads.

**Coach notes**

- This is the easiest of the 50 to run and the safest bet for a class you have not met.
- Directly mirrors the Monday Conditioning E2OM the members already understand.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, sled, box, dumbbell, wallball.*

### GD-27 · Merry Go Round

**Pairs** · 10 pairs · 34 min · *Carousel*

**The hook.** The rotation is a jog, not a walk. The room is always moving.

**Set-up.** 10 stations, one per pair, arranged in a loop.

**How it runs**

- 3 min per station, 30 sec to jog to the next one as a pair.
- Pairs alternate inside the station however they like.
- 10 stations, one lap, 35 min. Last station is a shared 2 min max effort on any machine.

**Score.** Station totals plus the finisher.

**Lanes.** Base: 2:30 stations. Build: 3 min. Push: 3:30.

**Coach notes**

- Number the stations on the floor with tape. Do it once and it stays for every Saturday.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, runner, sled, box, dumbbell, kettlebell, wallball.*

### GD-28 · Beat the Beep

**Quads** · 5 quads · 32 min · *Carousel*

**The hook.** A target you must hit before the beep. Miss it and the quad owes a penalty.

**Set-up.** Each quad owns a rower, ski, bike and a floor square.

**How it runs**

- E90S for 30 min. Each 90 seconds one nominated quad member must hit a fixed target: 250 m row, 200 m ski, 18 cal bike, or 20 box step-overs.
- Rotate the nominated person every interval so everybody takes 5 turns.
- Miss the target and the quad owes 5 burpees, paid in the following interval by whoever is free.

**Score.** Intervals hit out of 20.

**Lanes.** Base: 200 m / 150 m / 14 cal / 15 steps. Build: as written. Push: 300 m / 250 m / 22 cal / 25 steps.

**Coach notes**

- Set the interval timer on the TV at 90 seconds and then say almost nothing for 30 minutes.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, box.*

### GD-29 · Bingo Board

**Trios** · 6 trios · 36 min · *Grid*

**The hook.** Pick your own path. Completing a line is worth more than completing squares.

**Set-up.** A 5x5 grid on the TV, each square is a small piece of work.

**How it runs**

- Squares are 60 to 90 second pieces: 200 m row, 15 cal bike, 20 wall balls, one sled length, 150 m ski, and so on.
- A trio claims a square by having all three complete it. Any line of 5 is a bingo.
- 32 min. Squares are worth 1, a bingo is worth 8.

**Score.** Squares plus bingos.

**Lanes.** Base: only one member needs to complete a square. Build: all three. Push: all three, plus a 10 burpee tax per square.

**Coach notes**

- Build the grid once and reuse it. Change the square contents to make it a different session.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, sled, wallball, box, dumbbell.*

### GD-30 · Grid Lock

**Quads** · 5 quads · 34 min · *Grid*

**The hook.** There is only one board and every square can only be claimed once. It is a race for the easy ones.

**Set-up.** A shared grid of 20 squares on the whiteboard.

**How it runs**

- 20 squares, 5 quads. Each square is a defined piece of work worth 1 to 5 points.
- A quad claims a square by completing it and crossing it off. First there wins it.
- 30 min or until the board is empty.

**Score.** Points claimed.

**Lanes.** Base: halve the work in each square. Build: as written. Push: add 25 percent.

**Coach notes**

- Have a marker on a string at the board. People will lose it otherwise.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, sled, box, dumbbell, wallball, kettlebell.*

### GD-31 · Connect Three

**Pairs** · 10 pairs · 30 min · *Grid*

**The hook.** Two pairs play noughts and crosses. You win a square by beating them to the work in it.

**Set-up.** A 4x4 grid per pair of pairs, played head to head.

**How it runs**

- Pairs are matched into 5 head to head games.
- Both pairs attempt the same square at once. Whoever finishes first claims it.
- First to 3 in a row wins the game. Best of 3 games in 28 min.

**Score.** Games won.

**Lanes.** Base: shorter squares. Build: as written. Push: longer squares, loser does 10 burpees per lost square.

**Coach notes**

- Only works with an even number of pairs. With 18 in the room, make one team a trio and give them a 10 percent handicap.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, box, dumbbell.*

### GD-32 · Wager Round

**Trios** · 6 trios · 34 min · *Wager*

**The hook.** Teams declare a target before the round. Hit it and you double, miss it and you get nothing.

**Set-up.** Each trio owns a machine and a floor square.

**How it runs**

- 5 rounds of 5 min, 1 min between. Before each round the trio writes a calorie target on the board.
- Hit or beat it and score double the target. Miss it and score zero for the round.
- Round 5 the machine is chosen by the coach at the last second.

**Score.** Total wagered points.

**Lanes.** Base: half credit for missing by under 10 percent. Build: as written. Push: triple for beating the target by 20 percent.

**Coach notes**

- Round 5 is the good bit. Do not tell them the machine until targets are locked in.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike.*

### GD-33 · Double or Nothing

**Quads** · 5 quads · 32 min · *Wager*

**The hook.** After each round you either bank what you have or risk it on a harder round.

**Set-up.** Each quad owns a rower and a ski.

**How it runs**

- Round 1 is worth 100 points if completed inside 5 min: 500 m row and 400 m ski, shared.
- After each round the quad chooses to bank and rest, or go again for double with 25 percent more work and the same time cap.
- 5 rounds available in 30 min.

**Score.** Banked points only.

**Lanes.** Base: 20 percent less work each round. Build: as written. Push: 40 percent more per round.

**Coach notes**

- Every team that goes all five rounds fails. Watch them learn that and enjoy it.

*Gear: rower, ski.*

### GD-34 · Sled Train

**Quads** · 5 quads · 34 min · *Sled*

**The hook.** Only two teams can sled at once, so the rest are racing to be next in line.

**Set-up.** 2 sled lanes shared, machines as the holding pattern.

**How it runs**

- Quads work a machine circuit (300 m row, 250 m ski, 20 cal bike, shared) to earn a sled turn.
- A sled turn is 4 lengths, one per person, back to back. Queue is first come first served.
- 30 min. Sled turns are the score.

**Score.** Sled turns completed.

**Lanes.** Base: lighter sled, 2 lengths. Build: as written. Push: heavier sled, 6 lengths.

**Coach notes**

- Load the sleds before class and do not change them. Two weights, pick a lane.
- Manage the queue yourself for the first 10 minutes, then it runs itself.

*Gear: sled, rower, ski, bike.*

### GD-35 · Tug of Sled

**Trios** · 6 trios · 30 min · *Sled*

**The hook.** Straight racing. Two trios, two lanes, the room watches.

**Set-up.** 2 sled lanes, head to head heats.

**How it runs**

- Trios are seeded into heats. A heat is 6 sled lengths, relay, head to head.
- Between heats, trios work a 5 min machine block to stay warm and score.
- 5 heats in 28 min, so everybody races 5 times.

**Score.** Heat wins, then machine calories as the tiebreak.

**Lanes.** Base: lighter lane. Build: standard. Push: heavy lane.

**Coach notes**

- Head to head racing is the single most engaging thing you can put in front of a Saturday class.
- Run the heats yourself with a start call. It feels like an event.

*Gear: sled, rower, ski, bike.*

### GD-36 · Sled Toll

**Pairs** · 10 pairs · 32 min · *Sled*

**The hook.** Every 5 rounds on your machine you owe the sled.

**Set-up.** 2 sled lanes, all machine banks live.

**How it runs**

- Pairs alternate 200 m efforts on their machine. Every 5th alternation, the pair pays the toll: 2 sled lengths each.
- If the lanes are busy, they hold a farmers carry until a lane opens. The queue is part of the workout.
- 30 min.

**Score.** Tolls paid plus metres.

**Lanes.** Base: toll every 7 alternations. Build: every 5. Push: every 3.

**Coach notes**

- Stagger the pairs' starts so the tolls do not all arrive at once.

*Gear: sled, rower, ski, bike, dumbbell.*

### GD-37 · Out and Back

**Trios** · 6 trios · 36 min · *Runner*

**The hook.** One person is always out on the runner and the team's work only counts while they are gone.

**Set-up.** 6 runners, one per trio, plus floor space.

**How it runs**

- One member runs 400 m. While they are running the other two accumulate reps on a shared floor piece: wall balls, then box step-overs, then dumbbell snatches.
- Reps only count while the runner is running. Rotate on their return.
- 3 blocks of 10 min, 2 min between, one movement per block.

**Score.** Reps accumulated.

**Lanes.** Base: 300 m or ski 300 m instead. Build: 400 m. Push: 600 m.

**Coach notes**

- Always offer the ski as the run substitute. Some members will not use the runner and that is fine.
- This punishes a slow runner in a funny way rather than a demoralising way. Name it at the brief.

*Gear: runner, ski, wallball, box, dumbbell.*

### GD-38 · Lap Tax

**Quads** · 5 quads · 34 min · *Runner*

**The hook.** Every lap costs the team something, so they have to decide how fast to accumulate them.

**Set-up.** Runners plus machines.

**How it runs**

- Quads accumulate 200 m runner laps, one person at a time, 30 min.
- Every 10th lap the entire quad pays a tax: 30 synchronised air squats.
- A quad may bank early and switch to machines at any time, but machine work is worth half.

**Score.** Laps plus half credit machine work.

**Lanes.** Base: 150 m laps, 20 squat tax. Build: as written. Push: 300 m laps, 40 squat tax.

**Coach notes**

- Good session for a mixed-ability room because the machine escape hatch is always there.

*Gear: runner, rower, ski, bike.*

### GD-39 · Run Club Relay

**Pairs** · 10 pairs · 30 min · *Runner*

**The hook.** A rolling relay where the machine bank is the waiting room, not the rest.

**Set-up.** 6 runners shared, 4 pairs on machines at any time.

**How it runs**

- 6 pairs on runners, 4 pairs on machines. Runners alternate 400 m, machines alternate 500 m.
- Every 6 min the groups swap banks completely.
- 5 swaps, 28 min.

**Score.** Combined metres, runner metres worth double.

**Lanes.** Base: 300 m legs. Build: 400 m. Push: 600 m.

**Coach notes**

- This is the one to run on a Saturday where the run club members turn up in numbers.

*Gear: runner, rower, ski, bike.*

### GD-40 · Last Team Standing

**Trios** · 6 trios · 32 min · *Showdown*

**The hook.** A rising target each round. Miss the cut and you are into the consolation bracket, not out of the session.

**Set-up.** All banks live, cut-offs called by the coach.

**How it runs**

- Round 1: 30 cal team total in 2 min. Round 2: 40 in 2 min. Rising by 10 each round.
- Miss the cut and the trio moves to the consolation bank, where they keep working for a separate score.
- 10 rounds max, 30 min with the transitions.

**Score.** Round survived, and consolation points.

**Lanes.** Base: targets rise by 5. Build: by 10. Push: by 15.

**Coach notes**

- Never let anyone actually stop. The consolation bracket is the difference between this being fun and being humiliating.
- Great last-Saturday-of-a-block session.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike.*

### GD-41 · Sudden Death

**Pairs** · 10 pairs · 28 min · *Showdown*

**The hook.** Short races, straight knockouts, everybody races constantly.

**Set-up.** Rowers and bikes, head to head.

**How it runs**

- Round robin of 90 second head to head pair races: one partner works 45 sec, tag, other works 45 sec.
- Pairs are matched by the board each round. 12 races in 26 min including transitions.
- Machine changes every 4 races.

**Score.** Races won.

**Lanes.** Base: 60 second races. Build: 90 sec. Push: 2 min.

**Coach notes**

- Highest energy session in the library. Also the most work for the coach: you are the race caller for 26 minutes.

*Gear: rower, bike.*

### GD-42 · Podium Finish

**Quads** · 5 quads · 38 min · *Showdown*

**The hook.** A long team chipper where the last 5 minutes decide everything.

**Set-up.** All banks live.

**How it runs**

- Part A (25 min): team chipper of 3000 m row, 2500 m ski, 150 cal bike, 100 wall balls, 8 sled lengths, split any way.
- Part B (8 min): whatever is left carries over as a time penalty, then a straight 8 min all-in max calorie effort, all four working.
- Highest Part B total on the podium.

**Score.** Part B calories minus carried penalty.

**Lanes.** Base: reduce Part A by a third. Build: as written. Push: add 25 percent to Part A.

**Coach notes**

- Part B with all four quad members going at once is the visual. Save it for a week with a good turnout.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, wallball, sled.*

### GD-43 · Snake Draft

**Trios** · 6 trios · 34 min · *Choice*

**The hook.** Teams pick their movements in draft order, and the order reverses each round.

**Set-up.** All banks live, a draft board on the whiteboard.

**How it runs**

- 12 movement cards on the board with point values. Trios draft one each round in snake order.
- 4 rounds of 6 min, 2 min between. You perform what you drafted.
- A card drafted cannot be taken again by anyone.

**Score.** Points from drafted cards completed.

**Lanes.** Base: lower rep targets on every card. Build: as written. Push: higher targets.

**Coach notes**

- The draft itself takes 90 seconds and members love it. Build the card list once and reuse.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, sled, box, dumbbell, kettlebell, wallball.*

### GD-44 · Countdown

**Quads** · 5 quads · 32 min · *Choice*

**The hook.** Work windows shrink every round, rest stays the same. The urgency builds itself.

**Set-up.** Each quad owns a rower, ski and bike.

**How it runs**

- Rounds of 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 min work with 90 sec rest between.
- Each round the quad must complete a fixed piece: 60 cal shared. It gets progressively less possible.
- Then the same ladder in reverse, 1 up to 5, with a fixed 40 cal piece.

**Score.** Rounds completed inside the window.

**Lanes.** Base: 45 / 30 cal. Build: 60 / 40. Push: 80 / 55.

**Coach notes**

- The reverse ladder is where they recover the session. Tell them at the halfway point, it lifts the room.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike.*

### GD-45 · Trade Places

**Pairs** · 10 pairs · 32 min · *Choice*

**The hook.** You cannot use the same machine twice in a row, so the room is constantly moving.

**Set-up.** All banks live, pairs may move freely.

**How it runs**

- Pairs alternate 90 second efforts. After each alternation the pair must move to a different bank.
- If the bank is full they do 10 burpees and go to the next one.
- 30 min continuous.

**Score.** Total converted points (1 cal = 10 m).

**Lanes.** Base: 60 sec efforts. Build: 90 sec. Push: 2 min.

**Coach notes**

- With 6 machines per bank and 10 pairs the traffic is deliberate. That collision is the session.
- Good in a week where you want people talking to each other.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, runner.*

### GD-46 · The Gauntlet

**Trios** · 6 trios · 36 min · *Choice*

**The hook.** Every trio must pass through all six, and there is a guard at each one.

**Set-up.** 6 stations in a fixed line, run as a gauntlet.

**How it runs**

- Stations: row, ski, bike, sled, box and dumbbell, wall ball. Each has an entry fee (a short piece) and an exit fee (a shorter one).
- Trios move as a unit. All three must clear a station before moving on.
- Two full laps, 34 min cap.

**Score.** Stations cleared.

**Lanes.** Base: no exit fee. Build: as written. Push: double exit fee.

**Coach notes**

- Trios moving as a unit means the slowest sets the pace. Say that out loud so the fast ones coach instead of sulk.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, sled, box, dumbbell, wallball.*

### GD-47 · Mystery Box

**Quads** · 5 quads · 34 min · *Random draw*

**The hook.** Nobody knows the workout until the clock starts. Impossible to pace, impossible to be bored.

**Set-up.** A sealed envelope per round, opened on the horn.

**How it runs**

- 6 rounds of 4 min with 1:30 between. The round's work is revealed at the horn.
- Rounds alternate between machine pieces and floor pieces.
- Round 6 is a repeat of whichever round scored the lowest across the room.

**Score.** Round totals.

**Lanes.** Base: each envelope carries a base line. Build: build line. Push: push line.

**Coach notes**

- Write the envelopes the night before. This is a session you cannot improvise on the floor.
- Round 6 being the worst round is cruel and very popular.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, sled, box, dumbbell, wallball, kettlebell.*

### GD-48 · Handicap Race

**Trios** · 6 trios · 32 min · *Showdown*

**The hook.** Teams are handicapped so everybody crosses the line within a minute of each other.

**Set-up.** 6 rowers, then 6 bikes.

**How it runs**

- Race 1 (12 min): 2000 m team row, relay in 250 m legs. Handicaps set from last Saturday's board, or from the first 3 minutes if it is a new group.
- Race 2 (12 min): 100 cal team bike, relay in 12 cal legs, handicaps adjusted from race 1.
- 4 min between.

**Score.** Handicapped finishing position.

**Lanes.** Base: the handicap is the scaling. Build: as written. Push: as written.

**Coach notes**

- Handicapping is the single best tool for a mixed room. Nobody finishes alone and nobody wins by default.
- Keep last week's numbers. This is where the tool's Game Day history earns its keep.

*Gear: rower, bike.*

### GD-49 · Chain Gang

**Quads** · 5 quads · 36 min · *Tag relay*

**The hook.** Work passes down the chain in one direction only. If one link stalls, the whole chain stalls.

**Set-up.** Each quad owns 4 adjacent stations in a line.

**How it runs**

- Station 1 rows 200 m, which releases station 2 to ski 150 m, which releases station 3 to bike 12 cal, which releases station 4 to do 15 wall balls.
- When station 4 finishes, everyone shifts one place down the chain.
- 3 blocks of 10 min, 2 min between. Block 2 reverses the chain direction, block 3 doubles station 1's piece.

**Score.** Complete chains.

**Lanes.** Base: 150 / 100 / 8 / 10. Build: as written. Push: 300 / 250 / 18 / 25.

**Coach notes**

- Everybody spends a block being the bottleneck. That is the lesson and it lands without you saying anything.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, wallball.*

### GD-50 · TAC Championship

**Trios** · 6 trios · 45 min · *Showdown*

**The hook.** Three short events, a points table, a podium. Run it at the end of a block or as a community event.

**Set-up.** Whole room. This is an event, not a class.

**How it runs**

- Event 1 (10 min): 'The Engine' - 2000 m team row relay, 250 m legs. Fastest time.
- Event 2 (10 min): 'The Floor' - AMRAP of 20 wall balls, 15 box step-overs, 10 dumbbell snatches, one person working. Most reps.
- Event 3 (12 min): 'The Finish' - sled length, 200 m ski, 15 cal bike per person, in order, as a relay. Fastest time.
- 6 min between events for scoring and recovery.

**Score.** 6 points for first down to 1 for last per event. Overall podium.

**Lanes.** Base: reduce each event by a third, separate base division. Build: as written. Push: add 25 percent, separate push division.

**Coach notes**

- Run two divisions if the room is mixed. Two podiums is better than one podium and twelve disappointed people.
- Take photos. Put them on the TV next week. This is the community engine, not just a workout.
- Budget 45 minutes and start on time.

*Gear: rower, ski, bike, sled, wallball, box, dumbbell.*
